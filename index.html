      How This Works

      228 topics drawn from your PMA Extempore Handbook (16 themes), each carrying the meaning, expected points, flip side and military relevance already worked out for you.
      Flow: draw a topic → think in silence for the prep window → speak for the allotted time while it transcribes live (Chrome/Edge) or you fill it in after → correct the transcript → get scored.

      Scoring (10 pts): Grasp of the topic (2) · Example/evidence given (2) · Balance / flip side acknowledged (2) · Military-leadership relevance (2) · Structure & delivery (1) · Time use & pace (1). Each is checked against this specific topic's own points, flip side and military-relevance text, not a generic template.

      Everything runs locally in this file — no server, no upload, no account. Chrome/Edge get live captions while you talk, using the browser's own built-in speech engine. Safari (including iPhone) doesn't have that engine at all, so instead use the Transcribe Automatically button after you stop — it runs an on-device speech-to-text model right in the page (downloads once, then cached). Either way, or if you'd rather skip both, just type up the transcript from memory and the rest of the drill works the same.

      Your log is saved in this browser only (localStorage) — it won't follow you to another device or browser profile. Export it to CSV if you want to keep a record elsewhere.

      Put It On Your iPhone

      1. AirDrop this file to your iPhone (or save it from Files/email) and open it — it opens in Safari.

      2. Tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen. It now sits as its own icon and opens full-screen, no address bar, no internet needed — everything on this page is baked into the one file.

      On transcription: Safari on iPhone doesn't support live captions while you talk (that's Apple's restriction, not this app's). Instead, after you stop speaking, tap Transcribe Automatically on the review screen — it runs a speech-to-text model directly on your phone (no server, no account, no API key) and fills in the transcript for you. The first time you use it, it downloads that model once (~75MB, needs a connection); after that it's cached on the device and works without one. Review and correct whatever it produces before scoring, same as with the live version.
      Built for one purpose: walk into the panel room having already taken the hit of hearing yourself back.
